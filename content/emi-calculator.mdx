## What is an EMI?

An EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount you pay your lender every month until a loan — home, car, or personal — is fully repaid. Each instalment covers both interest and a portion of the principal, structured so the loan is cleared exactly at the end of its tenure.

## How the EMI Calculator Works

Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate, and tenure in years. The calculator converts your annual rate into a monthly rate, then applies the standard amortization formula to work out a fixed monthly payment. It also breaks down how much of your total repayment goes toward interest versus principal.

## Worked Example

For a loan of ₹5,00,000 at 8.5% annual interest over 5 years, the monthly EMI comes to roughly ₹10,258. Over the full tenure, you'd pay about ₹1,15,496 in interest on top of the ₹5,00,000 principal — a total repayment of around ₹6,15,496.

## Why Use an EMI Calculator

Knowing your EMI before taking a loan helps you budget accurately, compare offers from different lenders, and understand how tenure length trades off against monthly affordability versus total interest paid. A longer tenure lowers your EMI but increases total interest; a shorter tenure does the opposite.

## Things to Keep in Mind

- EMI assumes a fixed interest rate; floating-rate loans may see EMI or tenure change over time.
- Processing fees, insurance, and other charges aren't included in this calculation.
- Prepaying part of your loan can significantly reduce total interest paid.
