## What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is India's unified indirect tax applied to the sale of goods and services, replacing a patchwork of earlier state and central taxes. It's charged as a percentage of the sale price, at rates that vary by category of goods or services — most commonly 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%.

## How the GST Calculator Works

Enter an amount and choose a GST rate. In "Exclude GST" mode, the calculator treats your amount as the base price and adds GST on top to find the total. In "Include GST" mode, it treats your amount as already including GST and works backward to find the base price and the GST portion.

## Worked Example

A ₹10,000 amount with 18% GST added (exclusive mode) comes to a total of ₹11,800, with ₹1,800 as the GST amount. The same ₹10,000 treated as GST-inclusive at 18% breaks down to a base price of roughly ₹8,475 and GST of about ₹1,525.

## Use Cases
