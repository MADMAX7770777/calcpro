## What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple screening measure that relates your weight to your height. It's widely used as a quick, general indicator of whether your weight falls into an underweight, normal, overweight, or obese range.

## How the BMI Calculator Works

Choose metric or imperial units, then enter your height and weight. The calculator divides your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in meters to produce your BMI, then maps it to a standard category.

## BMI Categories

- Below 18.5 — Underweight
- 18.5–24.9 — Normal weight
- 25–29.9 — Overweight
- 30 and above — Obesity

## Important Considerations

BMI doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat, so it can be misleading for athletes or very muscular individuals. It's a general population-level screening tool, not a diagnosis — if you have concerns about your weight or health, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian rather than relying on BMI alone.
