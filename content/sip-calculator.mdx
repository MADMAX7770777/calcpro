## What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount into a mutual fund at regular intervals — typically monthly — rather than investing a lump sum. This averages your purchase price over time and builds a disciplined savings habit.

## How the SIP Calculator Works

Enter your monthly investment amount, the annual return rate you expect, and how many years you plan to invest. The calculator compounds your monthly contributions at the given rate to project a maturity value, and separates that into total invested amount versus estimated returns.

## Worked Example

Investing ₹5,000 a month for 10 years at an expected 12% annual return grows to an estimated maturity value of roughly ₹11.6 lakh — of which about ₹6 lakh is your own contribution and the rest is projected growth.

## Important Considerations

SIP returns are not guaranteed; the rate you enter is an assumption, and actual mutual fund performance depends on market conditions. Longer investment periods generally smooth out short-term volatility, which is one of the core benefits of investing via SIP rather than timing the market.
