## What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator works out exactly how old you are — down to the year, month, and day — based on your date of birth and a reference date. It's useful for eligibility checks, form filling, or just satisfying curiosity about milestones like your 10,000th day alive.

## How It Works

The calculator subtracts your date of birth from the calculation date (which defaults to today), carrying over days into months and months into years the same way you would by hand. It also converts your age into total months, weeks, and days, and tells you how many days remain until your next birthday.

## Worked Example

If you were born on 15 March 1995 and calculate your age as of 15 September 2026, you'd be 31 years, 6 months, and 0 days old — or about 11,506 days.

## Use Cases

- Checking eligibility for age-restricted services or exams.
- Calculating a child's exact age for medical or school forms.
- Finding out how many days until a birthday or anniversary.
